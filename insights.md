# Netflix Data Insights (Power BI Analysis)

## Overview

This dataset contains Netflix content including Movies and TV Shows. The goal of the analysis is to understand content distribution, trends, and basic patterns.

---

## Key Metrics

* Total Titles: 5747
* Total Movies: 5558
* Total TV Shows: 187

`Movies make up the majority of Netflix content.`

---

## Content Distribution

* Movies dominate the platform heavily.
* TV Shows are comparatively very limited.
* This shows Netflix has more focus on movie content in this dataset.

---

## General Insights

* Netflix library is heavily skewed toward Movies.
* TV Shows represent a small fraction of total content.
* The dataset suggests a strong preference for short-format entertainment.

---

## Observations

* Data cleaning is important (duplicate or inconsistent values may affect counts).
* Always use unique identifiers (like show_id) for accurate counting.
* Formatting in Power BI may show values in “K”, but actual values remain unchanged.

---

## Conclusion

Netflix content is mostly movie-driven, with a smaller portion dedicated to TV series. This imbalance is clearly visible in the dataset and helps understand platform content strategy.
